How the hall pass works

Inside
Show hall pass → scan → she goes out. Destination and return time are already set in the app.
Outside
Show the same hall pass → scan → she is inside. Nothing to fill in.

Today

Movement is a paper register at the gate.

With the app

Students and admins check in and check out from one app.

The case Scenario 1 Scenario 2 Scenario 3 Scenario 4 Scenario 5 Scenario 6 Optional Scope Price
1

Normal outing

  1. The resident sets destination and return time. No special permission.
  2. The resident opens the hall pass at the gate.
  3. Gate staff scan the hall pass.
  4. Exit is recorded. The resident is now outside.

A normal outing. No special permission. Aisha Khan, B-204, is going to Maulana Azad Library. Expected return is 7:00 PM.

2

She comes back

  1. She shows the same hall pass.
  2. The scan records return. Nothing extra to enter.
  3. If she is late, the warden sees her on the late list.
  4. A parent SMS is sent, and another when she returns.

Expected return 7:00 PM. Grace ends 7:30 PM.

3

Special permission

  1. She chooses late-night or home going.
  2. She fills the form and sends it to the warden.
  3. The warden approves or rejects.
  4. If approved, she uses the same hall pass at the gate.

Two types: late-night, and home going.

4

Gate staff at the main gate

  1. Gate staff opens Scan QR.
  2. Gate staff scans the hall pass.
  3. The screen shows Exit recorded or Return recorded, with name and time.
  4. If the scan is invalid, the screen says what to do.

Night duty at the main gate. One action: scan.

5

Warden on duty

  1. Warden opens the hall register: inside, outside, late, pending.
  2. Warden can open the late list.
  3. Warden can open a resident’s record.
  4. Warden approves or rejects special permission on the card.

Four counts first. Then the people those counts refer to.

6

Guest stay

  1. The resident applies for her guest. Start date and end date, at least a day before, up to 7 days.
  2. The warden approves. Payment is marked at the hall office.
  3. Gate staff record the guest in, then out. Guests do not have a hall pass.
  4. The warden register shows guests in hall. That number is for dining.

Aisha Khan applies for her cousin Zara Ahmed. Fri 18 Sep to Mon 21 Sep. Paid at the hall office.

*

Optional: Emergency

A resident can alert the duty warden and the gate, and call from the phone. It is not a leave form.

Maroon is only used on these screens.

What we will build

  1. Ordinary outing (destination, return time, scan at gate)
  2. Return, including late return and parent SMS
  3. Special permission (late-night and home going)
  4. Gate scanning
  5. Warden register (inside, outside, late, pending, guests in hall)
  6. Paid guest stay (start and end date, at least a day before, up to 7 days, in and out at the gate)

Optional: emergency contacts on the resident phone.

With personal Apple and Google accounts, same as the ICACON Companion app, App Store and Play Store are ₹0. Running cost is then ₹8,000 a year.

India VPS: one 2 GB server in an Indian data centre is about ₹500 a month, so ₹6,000 a year. It runs the app backend, the database, and a nightly backup. 400 residents and 10 admins fit on this size.

Parent SMS: only if a resident is late. Two texts: one when grace ends, one when they return. About 8 late returns a night × 250 term days × 2 texts ≈ 4,000 SMS a year. Indian transactional SMS is about ₹0.20 each, about ₹800. We estimate ₹2,000 so a busier year still fits.
